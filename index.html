Rhetorical Reflection

The Original Artifact 1. What media artifact did you choose? 2. What is its original medium and format? 4. What is the artifact trying to communicate, accomplish, or persuade its audience to do?

The Transformation 5. What new medium or format did you choose? 6. Why did you choose this medium? 7. What did you have to change when moving the artifact into the new medium? 8. What did you have to add, remove, or reinterpret?

Medium & Meaning 9. How does the change in medium affect the artifact's meaning? 11. How does it change the artifact's rhetorical impact? 12. What can your new medium communicate particularly well? 13. What does your new medium make more difficult to communicate?

Your Choices 14. What specific design, visual, verbal, audio, or technological choices did you make? 16. If you could continue developing the project, what would you change or improve?
